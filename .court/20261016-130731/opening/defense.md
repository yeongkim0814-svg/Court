# Defense opening

**1. The earlier verdict's gaps are now filled.** The prior trial ended GUILTY 6-0 because "no product, sales channel, revenue basis, weekly hour cap or payout route for a minor was named." This case names a product ("a PS1/PSX-style graphics toolkit for Godot 4 called 'RetroForge'"), a channel ("the Godot store"), a price ("reference $9.99") and an hour constraint removed: "exams do not limit their hours."
**2. The product is narrow and buildable by a beginner.** The scope is pixelation, color quantization/dither, vertex snap, distance fog, three presets, a demo and docs. That is a bounded, shader-level addon, not "games and websites." The objection that the user has no experience is outweighed by the user's long runway: "about 19 weeks" against a plan written for 14 days.

**3. The plan is honest about risk and gated.** It "states there is no evidence yet of paid demand" and sets measurable gates: "10 conversations... 30 Lite downloads, 5 install-success feedbacks and 3 expressions of willingness to pay." A free Lite tier plus Pro lets demand be tested before real money is committed. Competitors at $4.99-$5.99 show a market exists, and the $9.99 reference is a test, not a promise.

**4. Downside is capped and consent is built in.** Budget is "up to 500,000 KRW," and the plan's final section requires "no spending, publishing, account creation or external sending without user approval" and confirming "minors' payout eligibility with a guardian." Most of the work costs time, not cash, and time is what the user has.

**5. The environment mismatches are fixable, not fatal.** The stale Termux assumption, USD pricing and unconfirmed Codex/Gemini are editing errors in a document written by GPT without the facts.
**Concessions.** The Godot store's rules, fees, paid-item policy and payout and age conditions are unverified. Age, guardian consent and payout route remain unstated. Godot is not installed in the container, so testing is unproven. Profit of 1,000,000 KRW is a target with no evidence. These are pre-launch gates to resolve first, not reasons to drop the plan.
