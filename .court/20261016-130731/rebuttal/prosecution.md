# Prosecution rebuttal

**1. Gaps filled.** Partly survives. A product and a price are named, but the prior verdict also required a sales channel, a payout route for a minor and an hour cap. The channel is unverified, the payout route and age are still unstated, and "exams do not limit hours" removes a limit without setting one.

**2. Narrow and buildable.** Does not survive. 19 weeks of runway is not skill. The user has no development experience, Godot is not installed in the container, and the record does not show that shaders can be rendered or tested there.

**3. Honest and gated.** Mostly fails. The gates count free Lite downloads and "3 expressions of willingness to pay", which do not show 1,000,000 KRW of paid sales. Competitors include a free or pay-what-you-want shader and $4.99 packs, which pressure the $9.99 reference. The record gives no sales or conversion data.

**4. Downside capped.** Weakly survives. The 500,000 KRW cap is real, but guardian consent is only a stated future step, not obtained. The cap also does nothing about the larger cost, the user's time spent on an unproven product.

**5. Mismatches fixable.** Does not survive as framed. The store is the biggest one: the plan compares itch.io prices while the user means "the Godot store", whose rules, fees and paid-item policy the defense admits are unverified. Codex and Gemini are also unconfirmed, and the plan depends on them.

**Concessions.** The defense concedes the store rules, payout and age, testing and the profit target are all unproven. Those are the conditions the prior GUILTY verdict turned on.

**Point for the jury: 1.** If the channel, payout route and age are still unknown, the case as stated has not fixed the gaps that sank the earlier idea, so it should not go ahead as described.
