## Charges that stuck
1. No named product, sales channel or basis for the 1,000,000 KRW target (5 jurors).
2. No weekly hour cap or rule that exams come first, so the cost in senior-year hours is unpriced (4 jurors).
3. Nobody has checked whether a minor can legally receive payouts, or the tax and age rules (3 jurors).

## What the defense got right
1. The 500,000 KRW is a ceiling, not a commitment, so the money at risk can stay small.
2. The Claude, Codex and Gemini setup lowers the cost of building and reviewing, though finding customers remains the hard part.

## Conditions for acquittal
1. Pick one product and one sales channel aimed at a defined group (for example other Korean students), with a revenue estimate behind the 1,000,000 KRW.
2. Set a fixed weekly hour cap that puts exams first, plus a stop-loss well below 500,000 KRW and a small first spend.
3. Confirm your legal age and a working payout route (for example a guardian-held account), including tax duties, before any launch.
