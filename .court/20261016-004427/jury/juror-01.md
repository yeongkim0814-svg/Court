VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As a lawyer I see a probable minor (a high school senior in Korea is usually under 19) planning to take payouts, run a business and publish games with no one in the record having checked parental consent, platform age limits, tax or game-rating rules, so it should not go ahead as described.
DECIDING FACT: Both sides leave open "whether a minor can legally receive payouts" (prosecution's missing-from-record list; defense rebuttal point 5 calls it "a real unknown"), and nothing in the case addresses it.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case stated the user's legal age and a verified payout route (for example a guardian-held account or an adult-eligible platform, with tax and any business-registration duties confirmed) before any money or published product is involved.
