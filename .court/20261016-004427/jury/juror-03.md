VOTE: GUILTY
REASON: As an investor I would not fund a plan that names no product, no buyer and no way to earn, where up to 500,000 KRW is at risk against a 1,000,000 KRW target with no stated basis and an unpriced cost in a senior's exam-year hours.
DECIDING FACT: The defense itself conceded that the record does not say which games or websites, or how they would earn (ads, sales, subscriptions), and that the 1,000,000 KRW target has no stated basis.
WOULD FLIP IF: The case named a specific product, a sales channel and a revenue estimate behind the 1,000,000 KRW target, with a spending stop-loss well below 500,000 KRW and a fixed weekly hour cap that puts exams first.
