# Prosecution rebuttal

1. **Capped downside: does not survive.** A cap is not a benefit. The defense admits most side projects earn nothing, and the record names no product, channel or revenue source. The cap also ignores the user's time. The target has no stated basis.

2. **Time window: does not survive.** The defense admits it cannot show there is no conflict with study, and the record has no schedule. Its claim that the exam period ends in November is not in the record. "Until university entrance" may still cover exam preparation.

3. **Production setup: partly survives.** The Claude, Codex and Gemini setup lowers build cost. But the record names no game, site or buyer, and building is not the hard part of earning. Audience and distribution are, and no toolchain supplies them. The user is "not a professional developer," so they cannot easily judge output quality.

4. **Pays for itself and teaches: does not survive.** "Even partial income offsets" assumes income exists. The skills claim is a different goal from "profitability comes first."

**Concession:** The defense admits there is no plan, no monetization route and no basis for the target. That is the charge.

**Point for the jury:** The concession. Can a case with no product, no revenue route and no target basis "go ahead as described"?
