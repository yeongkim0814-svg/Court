2. **Weakly survives.** No traffic or conversion estimate is in the record, true. But 1,000,000 KRW is a modest target, and the plan's first cheap steps would produce those numbers.

4. **Survives, the strongest point.** The record gives no weekly hour limit and no rule that exams come first. It does not say the user would neglect them. The user set the period and is the decider.

5. **Partly survives.** The record is silent on marketing, payments, tax, and whether a minor can receive payouts. That is a real unknown. But silence is not evidence of inability, and the multi-AI setup does lower the cost of building and testing.

**The point for the jury: point 1.** Profitability comes first, and everything else turns on whether a goal with no product yet is a fatal flaw or a normal starting point. If the user can name a product and a sales channel, points 2, 3 and 5 shrink. Point 4 stands either way.
